# Data card

The bundled demonstration videos are locally rendered geometric/textured test
fixtures generated by `video_temporal_audit/fixtures.py`. They include ordinary
motion, injected flicker/freeze, cuts, transitions and camera-motion challenges,
plus timing/container cases. They are program material, not captured personal
footage or outputs of a video-generation model. The fixture manifest is the
source of case counts, labels and construction settings.

[FIXTURE_LICENSE.md](FIXTURE_LICENSE.md); code/documentation use [MIT](LICENSE).
This does not license third-party footage, model weights, upstream datasets,
Python dependencies or FFmpeg binaries. No model weights or FFmpeg binary are
bundled in this repository. Runtime binary terms depend on the actual build;
`docs/upstream_sources.json` preserves the earlier observed FFmpeg license.

Fixture event times and labels come from construction. They are not human quality
ratings or population ground truth. Uploaded/local user videos retain their own
rights, and HTML previews may expose their frames or metadata. An import being
accepted is not provenance authentication. See `docs/LICENSE_REVIEW.md` for the
scope of the repository inspection.

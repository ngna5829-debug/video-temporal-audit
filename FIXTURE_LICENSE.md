# Controlled fixture media

The geometric frames and synthetic MP4 fixtures generated by this project's
`fixtures.py` are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This dedication covers the original drawn geometry and generated fixture media
only. It does not change the licenses of Python dependencies, FFmpeg binaries,
upstream repositories, or user-imported media. No third-party footage or model
output is included in these fixture clips.

The v2 textured camera-motion and fade fixtures are also generated locally by this project and dedicated under CC0-1.0. They are controlled test renders, not model outputs or real-world footage.
